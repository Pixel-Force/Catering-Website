On the day

How the food runs on your wedding day

A typical 2pm ceremony. We agree every time with your venue and toastmaster.

  1. 14:45

    Drinks reception

    Canapés and soft drinks go out as guests leave the ceremony. 6–8 per guest over 90 minutes.

  2. 16:30

    Guests seated

    Allergy meals marked with names and served first.

  3. 16:45

    Wedding breakfast

    Starter, main and dessert, or sharing platters to every table.

  4. 18:15

    Speeches & cake

    We cut the cake and serve it with coffee, or as dessert.

  5. 20:30

    Evening food

    Bacon rolls, chaat station, pizza or a biryani pot for evening guests.

  6. 23:00

    Clear down

    We pack and clear the kitchen, and box any leftovers for you.

Wedding FAQ

Wedding catering questions

All questions
What deposit do you need?

25% to confirm your date, 50% eight weeks before the event and the balance 14 days before. Final guest numbers are due 14 days before.

Can you cater for halal, vegan and other diets?

Yes. We cook halal meat from HMC-certified suppliers, and vegetarian and vegan dishes are prepared separately. Every dish on our menu is labelled with its diets and the 14 allergens.

How do you handle allergies?

Tell us about allergies when you book. We label every dish with the 14 UK allergens, prepare allergy meals separately, label them with the guest's name and serve them first. Our kitchen handles nuts and gluten, so we cannot promise a dish is free from traces.

Can we have a tasting?

Yes. Weddings from 80 guests include a tasting for two at our Birmingham kitchen. Other tastings are £45 per person, deducted from your final invoice if you book.

Can you mix cuisines, for example British and South Asian?

Most of our weddings do. A common choice is canapés and a British starter, then a South Asian main course, with a dessert table that suits both families.

How far ahead should we book?

9–15 months for a summer Saturday wedding, 4–8 weeks for corporate and party events, and 48 hours for office lunches. Ask anyway if it's sooner: we often have weekday availability.

Questions

Catering questions, answered

The things people ask before they book.

All 14 answers
How much does catering cost per person in the UK?

As a guide, a drop-off buffet starts at about £16 per guest, a staffed hot buffet at £32, a sharing banquet at £44 and a plated three-course meal at £62, before VAT. Staff, drinks, hire and travel are added. Use the quick quote on our homepage for a typical figure for your event.

Is there a minimum number of guests?

Staffed events start at 20 guests and office lunch orders at £120. Private chef dinners start at 6 guests.

Do your prices include VAT and staff?

Package prices are per guest before VAT. Packages that say 'staffed' include the team; otherwise staff are £22 an hour each, with a 6-hour minimum. Your proposal lists every line.

What deposit do you need?

25% to confirm your date, 50% eight weeks before the event and the balance 14 days before. Final guest numbers are due 14 days before.

Can you cater for halal, vegan and other diets?

Yes. We cook halal meat from HMC-certified suppliers, and vegetarian and vegan dishes are prepared separately. Every dish on our menu is labelled with its diets and the 14 allergens.
